# Game Plan — Giraffe Maze

## The game in one sentence
You're a giraffe wandering a cozy maze, nibbling leaves off trees as you pass, until you reach the center where fireworks celebrate you.

## How it should feel
Cozy wonder. Relaxing, no pressure, satisfying. Not tense or fast.

## Who she plays as
A giraffe. Gentle, exploring. Loves eating leaves.

## Where it happens
A maze with trees along the paths.

## What you actually do
- Wander through the maze, finding your way to the center.
- Walk past trees and eat their leaves — the leaves disappear as you munch.
- Eating leaves is mostly for fun and satisfaction; it also marks where you've already been.

## What's in the way
Just the puzzle of the maze — finding the right path. No enemies, no timer.

## How you win / lose
- Win: reach the center of the maze — fireworks go off as a celebration.
- Lose: you can't lose. You just keep exploring until you get there.

## The shape of it
- One maze for now. Can add more (bigger, trickier) levels later.
- Each level would have fireworks at the center.

## How it looks
- Cozy, animal-themed. (Details still being decided — will react to first build.)
- Giraffe character. Trees with leaves that disappear when eaten.

## How it sounds
- To be decided. Cozy/wonder vibe suggests gentle, warm sounds.

## Who's playing
- Single player. Likely a friend on a laptop or phone.
- Want them to feel calm satisfaction and a little delight at the fireworks.

## Still deciding
- Visual style (pixel-y, hand-drawn, cartoony?) and main colors.
- Sounds.
- Whether eating does anything beyond fun/points.
- Giraffe's personality — does she say anything?

## Ideas for later
- More maze levels that get bigger and trickier.
- A reason to keep playing (unlock something?).
- A moment of surprise.

## What's working so far
- The giraffe can wander the maze with arrow keys or WASD (smooth easing,
  cozy pace, faces the way she walks).
- Hedges have leaves that fall away as she passes — a soft munch sound and
  little leaf particles. Shows where she's been.
- A glowing pink heart marks the centre; reaching it triggers fireworks and
  a "you found the heart" celebration.
- One maze, freshly generated each time. Bigger maze (21x15) fills the screen.
- Title screen with peeking giraffe and drifting leaves; any key/tap starts.
