# Make your own game 🎮

You're about to build a real game — one that runs in a browser, that you can
play, and that you can send to your friends with a link.

You don't need to know anything about computers. You just need ideas.

## How this works

You'll have a conversation. You'll get asked a lot of questions about your
game — who's in it, what you do, what makes it fun, what it looks like. You
answer however you want.

Then your game gets built, a bit at a time. You play it, say what you want
changed, and it changes.

That's it. You're the director.

## To start

Type this, or something like it:

> **I want to make a game.**

You'll get asked what kind of game you want to make. There are no wrong
answers.

If you already know your idea, just say it:

> **I want to make a game where you're a cat trying to knock everything off a
> shelf without waking anyone up.**

## If you're stuck for an idea

That's completely normal. Try answering one of these instead:

- What game do you already love? What's the best part of it?
- What's the most annoying thing about your day? _(Games are great at making
  annoying things funny.)_
- What would you want to be, if you could be anything for ten minutes?
- What's something you're really good at? What if that were a superpower?

Or just say **"I don't know, give me some ideas"** — you'll get some, and you
can pick one, mix two together, or say "no, but what about…"

## Things worth knowing

**You can change your mind about anything, at any time.** Halfway through,
you can decide the dragon should be a goldfish. That's allowed.

**Weird ideas are the best ideas.** A game about a snail delivering mail in a
thunderstorm is more interesting than "a jumping game." Go weird.

**Ask for anything.** "Can it have two players?" "Can I put my own drawing in
it?" "Can the music get faster when you're about to lose?" Just ask.

**If something looks wrong, say so.** "That's too fast", "I hate that colour",
"this is boring" — all really useful. It'll get fixed.

**Play it often.** The best ideas come from playing your own game and going
"ooh, what if…"

## Your plan

As you decide things, they get written down in **GAME-PLAN.md**. If you come
back another day and forget where you got to, just ask:

> **What did I decide last time?**

Have fun. Make something strange.
